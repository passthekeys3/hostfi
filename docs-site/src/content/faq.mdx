# Frequently Asked Questions

## What counts as a property?

Any rental unit you manage. It could be an entire house, an apartment, a single room, or a hotel room. Each property gets its own financial tracking.

## What's included in the free tier?

Up to 3 properties, manual expense and revenue tracking, 3 receipt scans per month, CSV export, email bill parsing, and basic anomaly detection. No credit card required.

## How does email bill parsing work?

Every account gets a unique email address (like `ab3kx92m@in.hostfi.ai`). Forward any bill or invoice to that address, and AI automatically extracts the vendor, amount, category, and due date. Review and confirm in your Inbox.

## How does receipt scanning work?

Upload a photo or PDF of a receipt (max 10 MB). AI reads the receipt and extracts the vendor, amount, date, and suggests a category. Free users get 3 scans per month; Pro and Business get unlimited.

## Is my financial data secure?

Yes. All credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access uses Row Level Security so you can only see your own data. See our [Security page](/docs/account/security) for full details.

## Can I export my data?

Yes. CSV export is available on all tiers. PDF export is available on Pro and Business plans. You can export expenses, revenue, and P&L reports.

## What PMS integrations do you support?

Currently: Hospitable, OwnerRez, Hostaway, and Guesty. Lodgify is coming soon. See [Integrations](/docs/integrations) for the full list.

## What happens if I downgrade or cancel?

Your data is never deleted. You revert to the free tier limits (3 properties, 3 receipt scans/month) and lose access to Pro/Business features, but all existing data stays intact.
