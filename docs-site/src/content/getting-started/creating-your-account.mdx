# Creating Your Account

Getting started with HostFi takes less than a minute.

## Sign Up

1. Go to [hostfi.ai](https://hostfi.ai) and click **Get Started Free**
2. Sign up with your email address, Google account, or GitHub account
3. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

That's it. No credit card required. The free tier gives you access to core features for up to 3 properties.

## First-Time Setup

When you log in for the first time, you'll see a welcome checklist on your dashboard. It walks you through:

1. Adding your first property
2. Adding your first expense
3. Setting up your billing email (for automatic bill parsing)
4. Connecting an integration (optional)

You can dismiss the checklist at any time and come back to these steps later.

## What's Included on Free

- Up to 3 properties
- Manual expense and revenue tracking
- 3 receipt scans per month
- CSV export
- Email bill parsing
- Anomaly detection (basic)

To unlock unlimited properties, PDF exports, Ask AI, Schedule E tax prep, and integrations, upgrade to [Pro or Business](/docs/billing/plans-and-pricing).
