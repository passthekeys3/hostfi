# Adding Your First Property

Properties are the foundation of HostFi. Every expense and revenue entry is tied to a property, giving you per-property financial tracking.

## Add a Property

1. Go to **Properties** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Property**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Name** — A friendly name (e.g., "Beach House" or "Downtown Loft")
   - **Address** — Start typing and select from the autocomplete suggestions
   - **Type** — Entire place, Private room, Shared room, or Hotel room
4. Click **Save**

Your property is now ready. You can start adding expenses and revenue to it.

## Property Limits

- **Free tier:** Up to 3 properties
- **Pro and Business:** Unlimited properties

If you reach the free tier limit, you'll need to [upgrade your plan](/docs/billing/plans-and-pricing) to add more.

## Importing Properties from a PMS

If you use Hospitable, OwnerRez, Hostaway, or Guesty, you can import your properties automatically by connecting your PMS account. See the [Integrations](/docs/integrations) section for setup guides.

## Editing or Deleting a Property

Click on any property card to open the detail view. From there you can edit the property details or delete it. Deleting a property does not delete associated expenses or revenue — those records are preserved.
