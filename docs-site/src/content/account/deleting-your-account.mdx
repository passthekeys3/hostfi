# Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your HostFi account and all associated data at any time.

## How to Delete

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Click **Delete Account**
4. Confirm the deletion

## What Gets Deleted

All of your data is permanently removed, including:

- Profile and account information
- All properties
- All expenses
- All revenue entries
- All parsed bills and inbox items
- All integration connections and credentials
- All anomaly logs and alerts
- All receipt uploads
- Rate limit records
- Alert preferences

This covers all 16 database tables associated with your account.

## Important Notes

- **This action is irreversible.** Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
- If you have an active subscription, cancel it first in the [billing portal](/docs/billing/managing-subscription) to avoid further charges.
- Connected integrations (Google, Slack, PMS) will be disconnected automatically.

## GDPR Compliance

Account deletion is fully GDPR-compliant. All personal data and financial records are permanently purged from our systems.
