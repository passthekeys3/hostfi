# Profile Settings

Manage your account details and preferences.

## Updating Your Profile

Go to **Settings** in the sidebar to update:

- **Display Name** — Your name as shown in the app
- **Email Address** — Your login email
- **Billing Email** — Your unique @in.hostfi.ai address for bill parsing (this is auto-generated and cannot be changed)

## Alert Preferences

In Settings, scroll to **Alert Preferences** to configure which email notifications you receive. See [Email Alerts](/docs/integrations/email-alerts) for details.
