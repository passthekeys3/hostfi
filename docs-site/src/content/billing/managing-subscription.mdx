# Managing Your Subscription

All billing is handled through Stripe's secure customer portal.

## Accessing the Portal

1. Go to **Billing** in the sidebar
2. Click **Manage Subscription**
3. You'll be redirected to the Stripe customer portal

## What You Can Do

In the Stripe portal, you can:

- **Update payment method** — Change your credit card
- **View invoices** — Download past invoices
- **Change plan** — Upgrade or downgrade
- **Cancel subscription** — Cancel and revert to free tier
- **Update billing info** — Change name, address, tax ID

## Billing Cycle

- Subscriptions are billed monthly
- Upgrades are prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period
