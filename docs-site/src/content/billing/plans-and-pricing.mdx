# Plans & Pricing

HostFi offers three plans to fit your needs.

## Free

**$0/month** — Perfect for getting started.

- Up to 3 properties
- Manual expense and revenue tracking
- 3 receipt scans per month
- CSV export
- Email bill parsing
- Basic anomaly detection

## Pro

**$15/month** — For serious hosts who want automation.

Everything in Free, plus:
- Unlimited properties
- Unlimited receipt scans
- PDF export
- Ask AI (50 questions/day)
- Schedule E tax prep
- All integrations (PMS, Google, Zapier, Make, Plaid)
- Full anomaly detection with alerts
- Email alerts

## Business

**$49/month** — For portfolio operators and power users.

Everything in Pro, plus:
- Benchmarking (compare your properties to market averages)
- Slack integration
- Priority support

## Credit Card Processing

HostFi offers optional credit card payment processing for STR expenses:
- **Free tier:** 0.5% processing fee
- **Pro and Business:** 0.25% processing fee
