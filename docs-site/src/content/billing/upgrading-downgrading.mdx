# Upgrading & Downgrading

You can change your plan at any time.

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Billing** in the sidebar
2. Click **Upgrade** on the plan you want
3. Enter your payment details (handled securely by Stripe)
4. Your new features are available immediately

## Downgrading

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Manage Subscription**
3. In the Stripe customer portal, switch to a lower plan

### What Happens When You Downgrade

- Your data is never deleted
- If you exceed the free tier property limit (3), you won't be able to add new properties but existing ones are preserved
- Pro/Business features become inaccessible but the data generated by those features remains
- Receipt scanning limit reverts to 3/month

## Canceling

You can cancel your subscription entirely from the Stripe customer portal. Your account stays active on the free tier with all your data intact.
