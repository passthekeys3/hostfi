# Ask AI

Ask natural language questions about your financial data and get instant answers.

> **Pro feature** — Ask AI is available on Pro and Business plans.

## How It Works

1. Click the **Ask AI** button on your dashboard
2. Type a question in plain English
3. AI analyzes your actual financial data and responds

## Example Questions

- "What did I spend on cleaning last month?"
- "Which property has the highest expenses?"
- "How much revenue did my Beach House generate in January?"
- "What are my top 3 expense categories?"
- "Am I spending more on utilities this quarter vs last?"

## Limits

- **50 questions per day** per account
- **500 character** maximum per question
- Questions must be related to your STR financial data

## What It Can't Do

- Access other users' data
- Make changes to your account (it's read-only)
- Answer questions unrelated to STR finances
- Provide tax or legal advice

## How It's Built

Ask AI uses your real financial data from HostFi (expenses, revenue, properties) to generate accurate, contextual answers. It does not use demo data or make up numbers.
