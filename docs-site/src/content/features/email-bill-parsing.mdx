# Email Bill Parsing

Forward your property bills to HostFi and AI automatically extracts the vendor, amount, category, and due date.

## How It Works

1. Every HostFi account gets a unique billing email address (e.g., `ab3kx92m@in.hostfi.ai`)
2. Forward any bill or invoice to that address
3. AI parses the email and extracts the key details
4. The parsed bill appears in your **Inbox** for review
5. Confirm the details and it becomes an expense

## Finding Your Billing Email

Go to **Settings** to see your unique billing email address. You can copy it and add it as a forwarding address in your regular email, or just forward bills manually.

## What Gets Parsed

The AI extracts:

- **Vendor** — Who the bill is from
- **Amount** — How much is owed
- **Category** — Automatically suggested based on the vendor and content
- **Due Date** — When payment is due
- **Property Match** — Attempts to match the bill to the right property

## Reviewing Parsed Bills

In your **Inbox**, each parsed bill shows:

- The extracted details with a confidence score
- The original email content
- Options to edit any field before confirming

You can:
- **Confirm** — Creates an expense with the parsed details
- **Edit and Confirm** — Adjust any fields first, then create the expense
- **Reject** — Discard the parsed bill

## Tips

- Forward bills as soon as they arrive for real-time tracking
- The AI gets better at categorizing as it learns your typical bills
- Works with most utility companies, insurance providers, property managers, and service vendors
