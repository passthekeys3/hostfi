# Revenue Tracking

Track income from every booking across all your properties and platforms.

## Adding Revenue

1. Go to **Revenue** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Revenue**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Property** — Which property earned this revenue
   - **Platform** — Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, Direct, or Other
   - **Amount** — Gross booking amount
   - **Date** — Check-in date
   - **Nights** — Number of nights (optional)
   - **Payout Date** — When you received payment (optional)
   - **Confirmation Code** — Booking confirmation number (optional)
   - **Platform Fee** — Fee charged by the platform (optional)
4. Click **Save**

## Automatic Revenue Import

If you connect a PMS integration (Hospitable, OwnerRez, Hostaway, or Guesty), revenue from bookings is imported automatically. This includes:

- Gross booking amount
- Platform fees
- Cleaning fees
- Owner payout amount
- Check-in/check-out dates
- Platform attribution

## Search, Sort, and Filter

The Revenue page supports the same search, sort, filter, and pagination as Expenses.

## Editing and Deleting

Click any revenue row to open the edit modal. You can update any field or delete the entry.
