# Receipt Scanning

Upload a photo of a receipt and AI extracts the details automatically.

## How It Works

1. Go to **Expenses** and click **Scan Receipt**
2. Upload an image or PDF of your receipt
3. AI extracts the vendor, amount, date, and suggests a category
4. Review and confirm the details
5. The expense is created and the receipt is stored

## Supported Formats

- JPEG / JPG
- PNG
- WebP
- GIF
- PDF

Maximum file size: **10 MB**

## Free Tier Limits

- **Free:** 3 receipt scans per month
- **Pro and Business:** Unlimited receipt scans

## Tips

- Make sure the receipt is well-lit and the text is readable
- Cropping the image to just the receipt improves accuracy
- Digital receipts (screenshots or PDFs) tend to parse more accurately than photos of paper receipts
