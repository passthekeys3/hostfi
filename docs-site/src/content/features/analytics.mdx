# Analytics & Reports

Visualize your financial data with charts and insights.

## Stat Cards

The analytics page shows 4 key metrics:

- **Total Revenue** — All-time revenue across all properties
- **Total Expenses** — All-time expenses across all properties
- **Net Profit** — Revenue minus expenses
- **Profit Margin** — Net profit as a percentage of revenue

## Charts

- **Revenue vs Expenses** — Monthly bar chart comparing income and spending
- **Expense Breakdown** — Pie chart showing spending by category
- **Trend Lines** — Monthly trends over time

## Filtering

Filter analytics by:
- **Property** — View data for a specific property or all properties
- **Date Range** — Select a custom time period
