# Schedule E Tax Prep

HostFi automatically maps your expenses to IRS Schedule E line items, making tax filing straightforward.

> **Pro feature** — Schedule E tax prep is available on Pro and Business plans.

## How It Works

Every expense category in HostFi corresponds to a specific Schedule E line:

| Category | Schedule E Line |
|----------|----------------|
| Cleaning & Maintenance | Line 8 — Cleaning and Maintenance |
| Insurance | Line 9 — Insurance |
| Management Fees | Line 11 — Management Fees |
| Mortgage Interest | Line 12 — Mortgage Interest |
| Repairs | Line 14 — Repairs |
| Taxes | Line 16 — Taxes |
| Utilities | Line 17 — Utilities |
| Depreciation | Line 18 — Depreciation |
| Supplies / Other | Line 22 — Other Expenses |

## Viewing Your Tax Summary

1. Go to **Tax** in the sidebar
2. Select a tax year
3. View per-property Schedule E breakdown

Each property shows:
- **Line 5 — Rents Received** (your total revenue for that property)
- All expense line items with totals
- Total deductions
- Net rental income

## Tips

- Review your expense categories regularly to make sure they're mapped correctly
- The tax summary is a guide for filing — always consult a tax professional for your specific situation
- Export the data to share with your accountant
