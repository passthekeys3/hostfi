# Expense Tracking

HostFi tracks every expense by property and category, giving you a clear picture of where your money goes.

## Categories

Every expense is assigned to one of these categories:

| Category | Description | Schedule E Line |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Cleaning** | Cleaning services, supplies | Line 8 |
| **Insurance** | Property insurance premiums | Line 9 |
| **Mortgage Interest** | Mortgage interest payments | Line 12 |
| **Repairs** | Repairs and maintenance | Line 14 |
| **Utilities** | Electric, gas, water, internet, trash | Line 17 |
| **Management** | Property management fees | Line 11 |
| **Supplies** | Guest supplies, toiletries, linens | Line 22 (Other) |
| **Taxes** | Property taxes | Line 16 |
| **Other** | Anything that doesn't fit above | Line 22 (Other) |

These categories map directly to IRS Schedule E for tax preparation.

## Adding Expenses

There are 6 ways to add expenses:

1. **Manual entry** — Add expenses one at a time from the Expenses page
2. **Email parsing** — Forward bills to your unique @in.hostfi.ai address
3. **Receipt scanning** — Upload a photo or PDF of a receipt
4. **CSV import** — Bulk import from a spreadsheet
5. **PMS sync** — Auto-extracted from booking data (cleaning fees, platform commissions)
6. **Bank sync** — Auto-imported from your bank account via Plaid

## Search, Sort, and Filter

The Expenses page supports:

- **Search** — Find expenses by description or vendor
- **Sort** — Sort by date, amount, or category
- **Filter** — Filter by property, category, or date range
- **Pagination** — Navigate through large expense lists

## Editing and Deleting

Click any expense row to open the edit modal. You can update any field or delete the expense.
