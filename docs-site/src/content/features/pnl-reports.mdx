# P&L Reports

Generate per-property profit and loss reports for any time period.

## How It Works

1. Go to **Analytics**
2. Select a property (or view all)
3. Choose your date range
4. Click **Export**

## Export Formats

- **CSV** — Available on all tiers (Free, Pro, Business)
- **PDF** — Available on Pro and Business plans

## What's Included

The P&L report shows:

- Revenue by platform (Airbnb, VRBO, etc.)
- Expenses by category
- Total revenue, total expenses, and net profit
- Per-property breakdown

## Tips

- Export monthly reports for your records
- Use the CSV export to import into your accounting software
- The PDF export is formatted for sharing with accountants or partners
