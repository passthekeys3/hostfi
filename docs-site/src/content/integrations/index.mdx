# Integrations Overview

Connect HostFi to your property management system, bank, and productivity tools to automate your financial tracking.

> **Pro feature** — Most integrations require a Pro or Business plan. Free users can use Email Alerts and Email Bill Parsing.

## Available Integrations

## Disconnecting an Integration

You can disconnect any integration from the Integrations page. Disconnecting removes the connection credentials but **preserves all previously synced data** (properties, revenue, and expenses stay intact).
