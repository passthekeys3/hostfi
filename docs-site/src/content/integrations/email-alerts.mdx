# Email Alerts

Get notified by email when important events happen in HostFi.

## Setting Up

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Scroll to **Alert Preferences**
3. Toggle on the alert types you want to receive

## Alert Types

| Alert | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **New Parsed Bill** | A new bill was parsed from your billing email |
| **Anomaly Detected** | An unusual spending pattern was flagged |
| **Overdue Expense** | A recurring or scheduled expense is past due |
| **Weekly Digest** | Summary of the week's expenses and revenue |
| **Monthly Report** | Full monthly financial summary |

## Frequency

- New Parsed Bill, Anomaly Detected, and Overdue Expense are sent in real-time
- Weekly Digest is sent every Monday morning
- Monthly Report is sent on the 1st of each month

## Disabling Alerts

Toggle off any alert type in Settings. You can also unsubscribe from the link in any alert email.
