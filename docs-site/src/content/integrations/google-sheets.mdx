# Google Sheets

Automatically export your HostFi data to Google Sheets.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Integrations** and find Google Sheets
2. Click **Connect**
3. Sign in with your Google account and grant permission
4. Select or create a spreadsheet using the Google Picker

## What Syncs

Your expense and revenue data is exported to the connected spreadsheet. Data syncs automatically when new expenses are confirmed.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** on the Google Sheets card. Your spreadsheet data is not deleted.
