# Zapier

Connect HostFi to thousands of apps through Zapier webhooks.

## Setting Up

1. Go to **Integrations** and find Zapier
2. Click **Connect**
3. Copy your unique webhook URL
4. In Zapier, create a new Zap and use **Webhooks by Zapier** as the trigger
5. Paste your HostFi webhook URL

## Available Events

HostFi sends webhook events for:

- **expense.created** — New expense added
- **expense.updated** — Expense modified
- **revenue.created** — New revenue entry
- **anomaly.detected** — Spending anomaly flagged
- **bill.parsed** — New bill parsed from email

## Use Cases

- Send expense data to Google Sheets or Airtable
- Get Slack or Discord notifications on specific events
- Log expenses to your accounting software
- Create custom workflows with any Zapier-supported app

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** to deactivate the webhook. No more events will be sent.
