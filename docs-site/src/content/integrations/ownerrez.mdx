# OwnerRez

Connect your OwnerRez account to sync properties, bookings, and revenue.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Integrations** and find OwnerRez
2. Click **Connect**
3. You'll be redirected to OwnerRez to authorize HostFi
4. Grant the requested permissions
5. Select which properties to sync

## What Syncs

- **Properties** — All properties from your OwnerRez account
- **Bookings** — Reservation details with financial breakdown
- **Revenue** — Booking amounts, platform fees, cleaning fees, payouts

## Real-Time Sync

HostFi receives webhooks from OwnerRez when bookings are created or updated, keeping your data current automatically.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** on the OwnerRez card. Previously synced data is preserved.
