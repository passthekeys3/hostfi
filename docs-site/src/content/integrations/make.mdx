# Make (formerly Integromat)

Connect HostFi to Make for advanced automation workflows.

## Setting Up

1. Go to **Integrations** and find Make
2. Click **Connect**
3. Copy your unique webhook URL
4. In Make, create a new scenario with a **Webhook** trigger
5. Paste your HostFi webhook URL

## Available Events

Same events as Zapier:

- **expense.created** — New expense added
- **expense.updated** — Expense modified
- **revenue.created** — New revenue entry
- **anomaly.detected** — Spending anomaly flagged
- **bill.parsed** — New bill parsed from email

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** to deactivate the webhook.
