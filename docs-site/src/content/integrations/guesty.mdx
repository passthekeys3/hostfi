# Guesty

Connect your Guesty account to sync properties, reservations, and revenue.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Integrations** and find Guesty
2. Click **Connect**
3. Enter your Guesty API credentials (Client ID and Client Secret)
4. Click **Save**
5. Select which properties to sync

## Finding Your API Credentials

In Guesty, go to **Marketplace > API** to generate your client credentials.

## What Syncs

- **Properties** — All listings from your Guesty account
- **Reservations** — Booking details with financial data
- **Revenue** — Booking amounts, fees, and payouts

## Sync Schedule

HostFi syncs with Guesty daily via an automated cron job. You can also trigger a manual sync at any time.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** on the Guesty card. Previously synced data is preserved.
