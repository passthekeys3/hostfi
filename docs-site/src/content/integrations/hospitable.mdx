# Hospitable

Connect your Hospitable account to automatically sync properties, reservations, and financial data.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Integrations** and find Hospitable
2. Click **Connect**
3. You'll be redirected to Hospitable to authorize HostFi
4. Grant the requested permissions (property, reservation, and financial data access)
5. You'll be redirected back to HostFi
6. Select which properties you want to sync

## What Syncs

### Properties
All properties from your Hospitable account are imported. You can choose which ones to track in HostFi.

### Reservations & Revenue
For each booking, HostFi imports:
- Gross booking amount
- Platform fees
- Cleaning fees
- Owner payout
- Check-in and check-out dates
- Platform (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, etc.)
- Confirmation code

### Expenses
Booking-related costs are automatically extracted as expenses:
- Cleaning fees (categorized as Cleaning)
- Platform commissions (categorized as Management)

## Real-Time Sync

HostFi receives webhooks from Hospitable for:
- New reservations
- Updated reservations
- Property changes

This means your data stays up to date automatically without manual syncing.

## Manual Sync

You can trigger a manual sync at any time from the integration card on the Integrations page.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** on the Hospitable integration card. This removes the connection but all previously synced properties, revenue, and expenses are preserved.
