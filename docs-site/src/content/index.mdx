# Welcome to HostFi

HostFi is an AI-powered expense management platform built specifically for short-term rental (STR) hosts. Whether you manage one Airbnb or a portfolio of properties, HostFi automates the tedious parts of financial tracking so you can focus on hosting.

## What HostFi Does

- **Expense Tracking** — Categorize and track every expense by property
- **Revenue Tracking** — Log income from Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and direct bookings
- **Email Bill Parsing** — Forward bills to your unique HostFi email and AI extracts the details
- **Receipt Scanning** — Snap a photo of a receipt and AI categorizes it instantly
- **Schedule E Tax Prep** — Your expenses automatically mapped to IRS Schedule E line items
- **Ask AI** — Ask natural language questions about your finances
- **PMS Integrations** — Connect Hospitable, OwnerRez, Hostaway, or Guesty to auto-sync data
- **Anomaly Detection** — Get alerted when spending looks unusual
